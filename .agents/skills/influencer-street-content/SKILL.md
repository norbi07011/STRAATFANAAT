# Influencers and street content

Full plan with question lists: `E:/STRAATFANAAT-marketing/sonda-uliczna/plan-sondy-i-influencerow.md`.

## Local creators

- Target: Den Haag region, 5–100k followers, humour/street/football style, Dutch audience, steady views over the last 10 posts, clean history (no violence, racism, hate against other clubs).
- Find via TikTok/IG search (#denhaag #haags #070 #straatinterview #fcdenhaag), commenters on big Haagse accounts, directories as a start. Never guess who a named creator is: ask the owner for the exact @handle and verify.
- Offers, cheapest first: gift without obligation → affiliate code (commission per sale) → paid video with ad usage rights (30–90 days) → season ambassador.
- Outreach in Dutch via `straatfanaat-nl-voice`; the owner approves every message before sending.
- **Dutch rules (Reclamecode Social Media & Influencer Marketing):** any relationship (money, gifts, discounts) must be disclosed clearly at the start of the post (e.g. `#ad` first or the platform's paid-partnership label), regardless of follower count. The brand must inform the creator of these rules. Written agreement: deliverables, timing, fee, ad usage rights and duration, disclosure, content restrictions. No minors without parental consent.

## Street interview series ("070 VRAAGT")

- Kit: phone vertical, dual wireless mic, **mic flag/cube with the STRAATFANAAT logo**, host always wearing the brand (same host every week), shirts as prizes.
- Formats: Haagse quiz with shirt prize, opinion/light provocation, matchday predictions, reactions to new designs (doubles as design validation), "who deserves this shirt".
- Edit: best answer or reaction in the first second, question as on-screen text, Dutch subtitles, 15–40 s; publish via trial-reel ABCD tests (`instagram-growth-system`); best clips become ads.
- **Consent (portrait right):** recognisable interviewees must agree to publication; consent to film is not consent to publish or to use in paid ads. Record a short verbal consent on camera at the end of each interview (social media and, separately, paid ads); keep that clip in the archive. No web form or database. No minors without a parent. Delete on request. Never humiliate people; laugh with them.
- Matchday: no flares, fights, slurs or content against other clubs.

## AI influencers and AI UGC

- Default: real people first; the brand's edge is real 070 authenticity.
- Allowed: AI backgrounds/scenes with real product photos; a clearly fictional, labelled brand mascot (e.g. an animated Haagse stork); quick AI ad concepts for testing.
- Not allowed: AI "customers" presented as real buyers or fake reviews; person-swapping into someone else's viral video; clones or lookalikes of real creators; AI-generated product images that differ from the real shirt. Label realistic AI content as required by Meta and TikTok.

## Six ad formats that last (adapted)

1. Recommendation/gift ("Kocht dit voor mijn vriend…").
2. Problem → solution.
3. Wow detail (puff/3D print close-up, stretch test).
4. Unboxing with the receiver's reaction.
5. Objection answer ("€55 voor een shirt? Kijk wat je krijgt.").
6. Street poll where the answer turns out to be our shirt.
