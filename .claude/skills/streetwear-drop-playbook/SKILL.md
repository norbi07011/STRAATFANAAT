# Streetwear drop playbook (STRAATFANAAT)

Context: own factory in China, MOQ 30 per design, sample €20, unit cost about €10, retail €55 incl. BTW. Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` for current stock.

## Phases

| Phase | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Design vote | 3–4 weeks before | Show 3–5 blurred or partial designs, followers vote (stories poll, comments). Votes guide the order quantity. |
| 2. Sample check | when the sample arrives | Film the sample (unboxing, print close-up, stretch test). Approve only if print, label and fit are right. |
| 3. Teaser | 2 weeks before | Close-ups, back print hidden, "binnenkort". Push the waitlist (link in bio, WhatsApp). |
| 4. Countdown | last 7 days | Story countdown sticker, reveal the full design 3–5 days before, show the real stock number. |
| 5. Drop day | evening 19:00–20:00, preferably not during a match | Waitlist gets the link 1 hour early. Post reel + carousel + stories. Answer DMs fast. |
| 6. Sell-through | first 72 h | Story updates with true remaining sizes ("nog 4x M"). Repost buyers. |
| 7. After | week after | Customer photos, "sold out" post if true, decide on restock. |

## Order quantity rule of thumb

- First run of a new design: 30 (MOQ). Size split for an oversized men's tee: S 10%, M 25%, L 30%, XL 25%, XXL 10%. Adjust with real sales data.
- Restock only when 70%+ sold within 3 weeks, or the waitlist for it is more than about half of the planned quantity.
- Never restock the same design under the label "limited" without saying it is a restock.

## Honesty rules (Dutch consumer law / ACM)

- Scarcity claims must be true: real numbers only.
- "Was" prices only if that price was actually charged (lowest price of the previous 30 days).
- Waitlist counts on the site must come from real signups (`waitlist_signups` in Supabase), not hardcoded numbers.

## Timing calendar NL

Season start (August), derbies and home matches, Koningsdag (27 April), summer, Sinterklaas (5 December), Black Friday (we do not discount; use "Geen Black Friday. Wel een drop."), Christmas (gift angle, ship deadlines PostNL).

## Measure per drop

Revenue, pieces sold per size, sell-through % at 24 h / 72 h / 7 d, waitlist → buyer conversion, cost per sale from ads, margin per piece after shipping and payment fees. Write the results into `.agents/product-marketing.md` under "Wnioski".
