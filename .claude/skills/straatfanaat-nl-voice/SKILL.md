# STRAATFANAAT: Dutch brand voice

Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` first for products, prices and stock.

## Hard rules (check every text)

1. **Never** write "ADO Den Haag", "ADO" or reference the club crest. Allowed: FC Den Haag, FCDH, DH, 070, Den Haag, Haags, de stad, ooievaar, 1905, groen-geel.
2. Never present the brand as official club merchandise. If asked: "STRAATFANAAT is een eigen merk uit Den Haag."
3. No violence, flares/fireworks (vuurwerk, fakkels), fights or hostility toward other clubs or cities, especially in paid ads. "Hooligan" stays out of ads.
4. Only true claims: real stock numbers, real prices, real material specs. No fake scarcity, fake reviews or fake "was" prices.
5. Prices always in euros incl. BTW: "€55".

## Voice

- Haags, confident, short. Talk like a fan from the city, not like a company.
- Use **je/jij** (never u). Light Haags flavour where natural: "070", "de stad", "Haagse trots", "wij van de straat". Do not overdo slang or invent dialect; one local touch per post is enough.
- Write proper Dutch spelling (t/d rules, "hij wordt", "jij komt"). If unsure, choose the simpler sentence.
- Mix of short and medium sentences in normal paragraphs. No one-sentence-per-line "broetry".
- English streetwear terms are fine and normal in NL: drop, oversized fit, restock, limited, matchday, merch.

## Banned (AI tells)

- "Het is niet X, het is Y" / "Geen X, geen Y, geen Z" lists.
- "Ontdek", "Til je stijl naar een hoger niveau", "Laat je inspireren", "Must-have", "Next level".
- Em dashes, emoji at the start of every line, "Wat denk jij?" as filler.
- Rhetorical question + colon reveal ("Het resultaat? ...").

## CTAs that fit the brand

"Stuur een DM met je maat." · "Bestel via DM of WhatsApp." · "Zet je op de lijst, link in bio." · "Tag iemand die deze moet hebben." · "Welke pak jij?"

## Comment-keyword CTAs

For posts that feed the DM funnel (see `instagram-growth-system`): "Comment DROP en je krijgt als eerste de link." / "Comment MAAT voor de maattabel." Keep the keyword short, in capitals, one per post.

## Hashtags

Hashtags barely affect reach. Use 3–5 relevant ones and spend no more effort on them. Core: `#straatfanaat #070 #denhaag #haags #streetwear`. Context: `#fcdh #fcdenhaag #groengeel #haagsetrots #matchday #streetwearnl #nieuwedrop`. Never `#adodenhaag` or `#ado`.

## Output

For the owner, deliver the Dutch text plus a Polish translation underneath, labelled "PL (nie publikować)".

## Learning loop

When the owner corrects a text (word choice, tone, something "we never say"), add the rule to this file right away so it never repeats. Ask the owner for real examples of how they write and talk (old posts, WhatsApp messages, voice notes) and add short samples here.
