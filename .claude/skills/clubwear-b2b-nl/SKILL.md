# Club clothing B2B in the Netherlands

## Offer

- Custom design (the club's own crest and sponsors, supplied and approved by the club, so no licence problem), short runs from 30 pieces per design, techniques DTF/DTG/screen print/puff/3D.
- Strength vs. Jako, Hummel, Robey, Erima catalogues: own design instead of a catalogue shirt, fan merch next to the kit, price and lead time from our own factory, personal contact in the club's language.
- Always state: lead time (production + transport, fill in from the factory), sample policy (€20 per sample, sample before mass production), payment terms (deposit before production).

## Who buys

| Segment | Decision maker | Need |
|---|---|---|
| Amateur football clubs (KNVB, roughly 3,000) | bestuur, kledingcommissie, sponsorcommissie, jeugdcoördinator | training kits, tracksuits, warm-up shirts, supporter merch for the club shop |
| Youth sections | jeugdcommissie, ouders | affordable team sets, often sponsor-funded |
| Other sports (hockey, korfbal, basketbal, futsal, vechtsport) | bestuur | same |
| Supporter groups / fan clubs | organisers | supporter shirts, scarves, caps |
| Schools, companies, events | marketing, HR | event shirts, teamwear |

## Season timing

- Clubs decide in **February–May** for the new season (August). Start outreach in January.
- Second window: winter break (December–January) for training gear and Christmas merch.
- Tournaments and end-of-season events (May–June): one-off shirts.

## Finding clubs

- Club websites and the KNVB club search, Instagram accounts of clubs in Zuid-Holland first (Den Haag, Rijswijk, Delft, Zoetermeer, Westland, Leiden), then the rest of NL.
- Note per club: name, city, sport, number of teams (from the website), current kit supplier, contact person and e-mail, Instagram.
- Use `lead-finder` / `prospecting` for the list and keep it in a spreadsheet.

## Outreach (Dutch, short, personal)

Structure: 1 specific detail about the club → 1 sentence what we do → 1 concrete proof (photo/sample) → low-friction ask (free design mock-up of their training shirt).

Example first message:

> Hoi [naam], ik zag dat [club] dit seizoen [concreet detail, bv. een nieuw meidenteam heeft]. Wij maken clubkleding op maat in eigen productie, vanaf 30 stuks per ontwerp, met jullie eigen logo en sponsors. Zal ik vrijblijvend een ontwerp maken van een trainingsshirt in jullie clubkleuren? Dan zie je meteen hoe het eruitziet.

Follow up after 4–5 days with the mock-up, once more after a week. Then stop.

## Pricing

Price = unit cost (from the factory, per technique) + shipping/duties share + margin. Typical club pricing per piece is below retail: start around 2–2.5x landed cost for team sets, higher for fan merch the club resells. Show the club its own resale margin for merch sold in the clubhouse.

## Rules

- Never send messages, offers or invoices without the owner's approval.
- Logos and sponsor artwork only from the club itself, with written OK on the final design before production.
- GDPR: business e-mail outreach to club addresses is fine; include who we are and an easy opt-out.
