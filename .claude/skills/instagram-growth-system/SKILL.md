# Instagram growth system (STRAATFANAAT)

Built from 12 creator courses (see `marketing/wiedza/wnioski-z-filmow.md`). Write all Dutch copy with `straatfanaat-nl-voice`.

## Cadence

- Grid: at least 3 posts per week (aim for 13+ per month).
- Trial reels: 1–5 per day, **never more than 5 per day** (reach penalty risk).
- Stories: daily, 2–4.
- Cross-post every reel to the Facebook page (and the same file to TikTok, uploaded natively).
- First 15–20 posts are practice: fill the grid fast instead of perfecting post #1.

## Trial reels

- Go only to non-followers and first to people **near the poster's location**, which is perfect for a Den Haag brand. Post them from The Hague.
- Grid and trial reels are separate systems: the same file may go to both without a duplicate penalty.
- **ABCD hook test**: film the body once, export it with captions, then attach 2–4 different openings (different first 2–3 seconds or different on-screen text). Post them as trial reels about 5 minutes apart. Same caption is fine.
- Promote the winner: (a) "share to profile" from manage trial reels for experimental formats, so it arrives with social proof, or (b) post the file fresh to the grid when it clearly fits the audience.
- **Timing rule**: promote within 48 h, or before daily view growth drops below 25% of the previous day. Too late means it dies on the grid.
- Recycle winners after 60 days. Variants that count as new: new text hook, zoom 1.02x, speed 1.02x.
- Judge by: views, average watch time, skip rate, saves, shares, comment rate and a retention graph that flattens after the first drop.

## Comment-keyword DM funnel

- In a reel/carousel/story: "Comment DROP en je krijgt als eerste de link." Use a short keyword per campaign (DROP, KEIZER, MAAT, 070).
- Automation (Meta Business Suite automations or ManyChat): ask for a follow → ask e-mail or WhatsApp → send the promised thing (waitlist, early access, size chart, drop time) with a tracking link (UTM).
- Store contacts in the owner's own list (Supabase `waitlist_signups` / newsletter), not only inside Instagram.
- Do not put a keyword CTA on every post. Mix in pure vibe/personality posts.
- Stories convert better than feed posts: repeat the CTA in stories.
- Setting up automations or sending DMs needs the owner's approval.

## People over product shots

- Brand accounts that only post product photos hit a ceiling. Every week include:
  - **Yap video**: the owner talking to the phone, no editing (factory, samples, why 300 gsm, failed tracksuits, design story, matchday). Film when the idea comes.
  - **ON HOLD (owner, 2026-11-07):** no owner face and no talking-to-phone videos until the account has an audience. Tell the same stories as voiceover (ElevenLabs, Dutch) over product, hands, real-model shots with no face and Hague B-roll. Ask the owner before bringing yap videos back.
  - **Hero's journey episode**: what happened in the business this week, shown and captioned.
  - **Fans and the city**: real people from 070 wearing it (with their permission).

## Funnel mix for a clothing brand

| Level | Share | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Top (reach) | ~50% | street/city/matchday culture, stunts, memes only 070 gets, trends |
| Middle (trust) | ~35% | fit breakdowns, how to style, close-ups, behind the scenes, founder story |
| Bottom (sale) | ~15% | drop announcements, price, sizes, "how to order", keyword CTA |

## Topic altitude (bullseye)

1. Very narrow: FCDH die-hards (converts best, small reach).
2. Den Haag / 070 identity.
3. Dutch football and supporter culture.
4. Dutch streetwear and street style.
5. Broad "chaos" tests: anything with mass appeal (reach spikes).

Plan most posts on levels 1–3, one or two experiments per week on 4–5.

## Formats

Format = story structure × visual execution. Structures: story, problem-solution, list, Q&A, before/after, behind the scenes, reaction. Executions: talking head, street walk, close-up macro, POV, text-on-video, carousel, photo dump. Test 2–3 formats per round of 7 posts. Every reel has a spoken hook and an on-screen text hook.

## Ideas

- Idea quality > hook > format. Hashtags barely matter (use 3–5 relevant ones, no effort beyond that).
- Keep a swipe file `marketing/wiedza/swipe-file.md`: links to top posts from streetwear and supporter brands, each with "why it works" (hook, structure, emotion). Reverse-engineer, never copy.
- Mix validated ideas (already working in the niche) with original ones (only we can show: own factory, 070, Polish roots). Borrow ideas from other platforms.
- Hook levers: specific numbers, pattern interrupt, negative framing, call out the person ("Voor iedereen uit 070"), name the problem, benefit, proof, warning, confidence.

## Weekly review (every Monday)

Pull from Instagram insights (or the owner's screenshots):
- Top 3 and bottom 3 posts and why.
- Hook rate (3-second hold), average watch time, saves, shares, non-follower %, follows per post, profile visits, DMs, keyword comments, link clicks, signups, sales.
- Which topic level and format won. Plan next week: more of the winners, 1–2 new tests.
- Write findings into `.agents/product-marketing.md` → "Wnioski" and update this skill or `straatfanaat-nl-voice` if a rule changed.
